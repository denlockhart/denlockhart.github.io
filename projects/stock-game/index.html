    Rules

    • Each player begins with $10,000.
    • Buy a dollar amount of shares anytime during the day (fractional shares allowed).
    • Buy/sell prices are the stock’s prior close (last night’s end-of-day value).
    • At 6:00 PM Eastern on weekdays (after the 4 PM market close), the app refreshes prior closes and recalculates every player’s balance (cash + holdings). Shares stay open overnight.
    • You can sell holdings back to cash at the current prior-close mark.
